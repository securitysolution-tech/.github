# SecuritySolution.tech

Penetration testing, security monitoring and compliance readiness for companies in the
United Arab Emirates. You work directly with the people who test.

## What we do

- **Penetration testing.** We test your web apps, APIs, networks and cloud the way a real
  attacker would, then show you how to close every gap we find.
- **Security monitoring.** We set up monitoring that spots attacks early, prepare your team
  for incidents, and harden the systems attackers try first.
- **Compliance readiness.** We get you ready for the security standards your customers and
  regulators ask about, with policies and training your staff will follow.
- **AI and cloud security.** We test the AI features and cloud accounts your business now
  depends on, before someone else finds the weak point.

## Start with your own domain

Type your domain on [securitysolution.tech](https://securitysolution.tech/) for a
five-second read of what an attacker sees. It runs in your browser; nothing is sent to us.
