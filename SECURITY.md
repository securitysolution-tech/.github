# Security policy

If you find a security issue in one of our websites, systems or repositories, we want to
hear about it. Here is how to reach us, what happens next, and what stays out of scope.

## How to report

Email **security@securitysolution.tech** with a description of the issue, the steps to
reproduce it, and the impact you expect. Screenshots or a short proof of concept help us
move faster. Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

Machine-readable contact details are published in our
[security.txt](https://securitysolution.tech/.well-known/security.txt).

## What you can expect

1. **Acknowledge.** We confirm your report within 3 business days.
2. **Investigate.** We reproduce the issue and keep you updated while we work on it.
3. **Fix.** We resolve it and let you know when the fix is live.
4. **Credit.** We thank you publicly on our security page if you would like that.

## Scope

securitysolution.tech and its subdomains, including the domain check and the request form
on the site, and the code in this organisation's public repositories.

Out of scope: the services our hosting and DNS providers run (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare),
reports produced by an automated scanner without a working proof of concept, and issues in
third-party sites we link to.

## Please do not

- Access, change or delete data that is not yours.
- Run denial-of-service tests or high-volume automated scans.
- Use social engineering, phishing or physical attacks.
- Share the issue publicly before we have fixed it.

## Safe harbour

We will not take legal action against security research that follows this policy in good
faith. We do not run a paid bug bounty at the moment.

The full policy is at <https://securitysolution.tech/security/>.
